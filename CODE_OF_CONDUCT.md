# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TRex WebUI contributors, maintainers, and users commit to a respectful,
professional, and harassment-free community. We welcome participation
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

Positive behavior includes:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experience;
- giving specific, technical, and constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- protecting private lab, employer, and personal information; and
- prioritizing community safety over winning an argument.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or attention;
- trolling, insults, threats, or personal and political attacks;
- harassment in public or private project spaces;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, project events, and private communications made in a project role. It
also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For GitHub-hosted abuse, use GitHub's built-in content reporting and abuse
reporting tools. For a project conduct incident, contact an active maintainer
through a private contact method that the maintainer publishes on their GitHub
profile. Do not put personal, sensitive, or identifying details in a public
issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately as practical, disclose conflicts
of interest, and recuse themselves when appropriate. They may remove content,
issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
exclude a participant depending on impact, context, and repeated behavior.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is
prohibited.

This policy applies equally to maintainers. A maintainer who violates it may
lose moderation or repository access.
